# Stackfall Stage Visual Canon — 2026-10-16

> **Status: DORMANT STAGE REFERENCE.** Stage gameplay is currently sealed. This visual reference does not activate Stage runtime or override current Endless behavior.


This document supersedes earlier per-stage/story-background art direction.

## Canonical rule

- Endless mode is the visual baseline for normal space gameplay.
- Authored stages use the same space rendering language and background construction as Endless.
- A stage does not receive a bespoke story backdrop.
- Background identity changes at the constellation level, not the individual stage level.
- Stages inside one constellation share the same palette family and background-object vocabulary.
- Stage identity should come primarily from encounter composition, gimmicks, minibosses, bosses, events, pacing, and audio rather than a separate world-art style.

## Constellation background families

1. Constellation 01: blue-violet / cyan, broken shard silhouettes, incomplete orbital guides.
2. Constellation 02: magenta / teal, soft stellar-growth curves and drifting spore-like points.
3. Constellation 03: teal / muted amber, sparse relay geometry, nodes, and scan lines.

All three retain the Endless star field, nebulae, depth/parallax logic, and dark-cosmic composition underneath these variations.

## Stage 1 remodel

- Stage 1 is the first active authored runtime after the redesign reset.
- Display name: `외곽 항로 / Outer Route`.
- Final boss: `궤도 감시자 / Orbit Warden`.
- The discarded Closure/폐쇄선 visual concept is not canonical and must not be restored.
- The final boss deliberately reuses the established regular-boss geometric/orbital language, then adds a small Stage 1-specific broken-orbit signature instead of introducing a separate art style.
